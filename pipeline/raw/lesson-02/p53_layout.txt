Work Together
                                                Making an Artwork with a Scenic Photo

STEP

1
        Find some scenic photos out of which you could make an artwork.

        e.g. I think a photo of Mt. Everest would be good for our project.




STEP

2
        As a group of three, discuss which photo would be best for making an artwork.
                                                                                        e.g.
          A   Which photo would be best for our project?
          B   How about this mountain covered with snow?
          C   Good! It reminds me of patbingsu, a bowl of shaved ice with red beans.
          A    I agree with you. What can we add to this photo?
          B    Let’s draw a bowl, red beans, and other toppings.



STEP

3
        Add an illustration to the scenic photo that you chose.




                     My group changed Mount Everest into patbingsu , a bowl of shaved ice
                   with red beans. We thought the snow on Mount Everest looked similar to
                   shaved ice in a bowl. This reminded us of patbingsu . Thank you.



STEP

4
        Share your group’s work on the class website and give feedback on other groups’
        works.
