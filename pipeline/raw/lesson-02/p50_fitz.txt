The Dancing House
My favorite piece of architecture is the Dancing House in Prague, 
Czech Republic. Here is some interesting information about it. 
First, the Dancing House, which was completed in 1996, was a 
collaboration between Canadian American architect Frank Gehry 
and Croatian Czech architect Vlado Milunic´. Furthermore, it 
is a complex structure constructed of various materials such as 
concrete, steel, and glass, and the majority of the structure is made 
up of two towers. Lastly, its unusual shape reminds us of Ginger 
and Fred, the famous dance partners from Hollywood movies. It 
looks like a woman and a man holding hands and dancing together.
Someday, I would like to visit the Dancing House to fully 
appreciate the architects’ innovative designs.
