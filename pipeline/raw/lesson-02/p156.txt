My favorite artwork is Sky Above Clouds
IV by Georgia O’Keeffe. Here is some
interesting information about it.
First, Sky Above Clouds IV , painted in
1965, offers an impressive description of
the sky seen from a plane. The painting
captures the painter’s unique perspective of
flying high above the earth. O’Keeffe used
gentle blue and white colors to convey the
peaceful atmosphere. Furthermore, Sky
Above Clouds IV isn’t just a representation
of what O’Keeffe saw. The artwork reveals
