Step 3  Drafting
Sample  
My favorite artwork is Sky Above Clouds 
IV by Georgia O’Keeffe. Here is some 
interesting information about it.
First, Sky Above Clouds IV, painted in 
1965, offers an impressive description of 
the sky seen from a plane. The painting 
captures the painter’s unique perspective of 
flying high above the earth. O’Keeffe used 
gentle blue and white colors to convey the 
peaceful atmosphere. Furthermore, Sky 
Above Clouds IV isn’t just a representation 
of what O’Keeffe saw. The artwork reveals 
the emotions that come from observing the 
sky from high above the earth’s surface, so 
it is full of sense of wonder and calmness. 
Lastly, a lot of people love this painting 
because it is not like other art. Through the 
painting, O’Keeffe not only captures the 
elements of nature, but also invites viewers 
to appreciate its beauty in an innovative way.
I hope you found this information 
fascinating, and I highly recommend 
exploring this fine piece of art further if you 
have the opportunity!
