Six Strings
Six Strings is a cool place where students can learn to play the 
guitar, discuss music, show their skills, and teach others.

could get a chance to play on stage. How exciting is that? The 
club also sometimes participates in community events. 
You don’t have to bring a guitar; all you need is the willingness 
to interact with others.
Don’t you want to make new friends while having fun playing 
the guitar and singing along? Come on and sign up now! You 
won’t regret it.
